GIFOX is made by Iyanuoluwa Eyinade ("we"). This policy explains what the GIFOX app, its keyboard, share extension and iMessage stickers do with your information. In short: there are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Most of what you do in GIFOX never leaves your iPhone.

What stays on your iPhone

Your GIFs, stickers, favourites, folders, clipboard history, words the keyboard learns, settings and any API keys you add are stored only on your iPhone (in GIFOX's app group and Keychain). We can't see them. Deleting GIFOX deletes them.

What you type: the keyboard never sends your keystrokes anywhere. Text only leaves your iPhone when you choose one of the features below.

Our AI server

The GIFOX Plus AI server does not store the text you send or the answers. To apply the daily limit it keeps a counter for your subscription's anonymous transaction number for one day. OpenAI processes requests under its API terms: it does not use API data to train its models and may keep it for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring (OpenAI API data use). Vercel may log technical request data (time, IP address) for security, as hosting providers do.

Purchases

GIFOX Plus is sold by Apple. Apple handles payment; we never see your card or Apple ID. The app checks your subscription with Apple's StoreKit, and the AI server checks Apple's signature on your subscription.

Photos and the clipboard

GIFOX only reads the photos and videos you pick. The keyboard reads the clipboard only when you tap Paste or Add (or if you turn on "Save copies automatically"). Copies marked as passwords are skipped, and short number codes are never kept.

Children

GIFOX is not directed to children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

You can use GIFOX without GIF search, online translation or AI. Turn off Allow Full Access to keep the keyboard fully offline. Clear clipboard history in GIFOX → Clipboard. Because we don't hold personal data on our servers, there is nothing for us to delete, but you can email us with any question.

Changes and contact
