Turn on the keyboard

  1. Open Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard and pick GIFOX.
  2. Tap GIFOX and turn on Allow Full Access. Typing works without it; your GIFs, GIF search, clipboard, translation and AI need it.
  3. In any chat, hold the 🌐 key and choose GIFOX.

Paste does nothing, or iOS keeps asking “Allow Paste?”

iOS can't show the paste question over a keyboard. Open Settings → Apps → GIFOX → Paste from Other Apps and choose Allow.

Sending a GIF

Keyboards can only type text, so GIFOX copies the GIF: tap it, then tap the message box and choose Paste. If a GIF arrives frozen, hold it in GIFOX and choose Copy as video.

GIFOX Plus

GIFOX Plus adds ChatGPT writing help in the keyboard (no API key needed). Apple Intelligence stays free. Manage or cancel in Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Restore a purchase in GIFOX → Settings → GIFOX Plus → Restore purchases.
